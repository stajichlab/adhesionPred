## 2. Decisions taken (owner, 2026-10-06)

1. R0 leakage: only tuning of R0's own cutoffs and overlap with R0's own reference set cap a status. SignalP 6
   training overlap with the Phase C positives is **not measured**; it is a stated limit, not a cap.
2. `calibrate phasec` records cluster counts and applies the widest-of interval rule (done).
3. A call's status comes from a measurement of that call. `calibrate truth` refuses a call that reads more than
   one module (done). Per-call status entries need a change to the status file format (not done).
4. Module identities go into `run.json`; `truth` and `phasec` refuse a mismatch (done).
5. A composite call takes the weakest status of the modules that decided it (kept).
6. UniProt `Repeat` features are an independent evidence tier for "has a tandem repeat region", kept separate
   from paper statements and from family inference.
7. Ties between the two labelling agents are broken by evidence tier, not by agent. The two rows that the rule
   cannot settle go to a human expert.
8. "Phase C" is the held-out evaluation of step 1. A better name for future text is "step 1 held-out benchmark".
   No rename now.

Not decided: whether family-level inference may count as positive control for the repeat call (the three tiers
are reported side by side until you decide).
