# PF28404 (ARB_05178) family: distribution and tree in Onygenales

Date: 2026-10-02. Branch: `pf28404-family`. Analysis only. No function is inferred.

## Question

PRA3 (CIMG_02492) is a *Coccidioides* protein with 12 Cys and no known function. Pfam family
PF28404 (ARB_05178) contains PRA3. The `cys_candidates` run found three more PF28404 proteins in
every *Coccidioides* proteome (CIMG_07303, CIMG_05560, CIMG_07843). Questions:

1. How are these copies related to each other?
2. Does the family occur in other Onygenales, and in how many copies?
3. Is the family specific to *Coccidioides*?

## What Pfam says

From the InterPro API, 2026-10-02:

- PF28404 has no clan and no GO term. It has no experimental structure.
  It has 699 AlphaFold models.
- InterPro lists 842 proteins in 557 proteomes (1,126 taxa) and 6 domain architectures.
- The description is text for the 210 aa prototype from *Arthroderma benhamiae*, UniProt
  D4ALI1 (Swiss-Prot entry A5178_ARTBC). It has a signal peptide, is predicted to be N-glycosylated,
  and has a region of predicted disorder (residues 125 to 186). The function is unknown. The
  description is marked as not manually checked.

## Methods

Scripts are in `analysis/pf28404_family/`. They need `WORK` and other paths from the
environment, not from the script location.

1. `00_select_proteomes.py`. 831 proteomes: all 624 Fungi_5k Eurotiomycetes proteomes (483
   Eurotiales, 63 Chaetothyriales, 78 Onygenales with the seven *Coccidioides* references of
   `cys_candidates`, and others), plus 40 random proteomes (fixed seed 20261002) from each of five
   other Pezizomycotina classes as an outgroup check. *Coccidioides* pangenome strains were not
   searched. They add strain copies, not new family members.
2. `01_hmmsearch.sh`. HMMER 3.4 `hmmsearch --cut_ga` of the PF28404.1 model (GA 27.0) against each
   proteome. SLURM job 29351176 on `highclock`, 8 s. A control: *C. immitis* RS gives 4 hits, as in
   the `cys_candidates` README.
3. `02_collect_hits.py`. One row per protein (best domain): 408 proteins in 283 of 831 proteomes.
4. `03_select_tree_tips.py`. 217 tips: all 166 Onygenales hits of 400 aa or less, plus the
   best hit of up to 4 random proteomes of every other order that has hits. One Onygenales hit is
   longer than 400 aa and is not in the tree.
5. `04_tree.sh`. MAFFT 7.505 `--auto`, trimAl 1.4 `-automated1`, IQ-TREE 2.2.2.6 with ModelFinder
   (best model by BIC: Q.pfam+I+R5) and 1,000 ultrafast bootstrap replicates. SLURM job 29351178,
   8 min 48 s. The alignment has 884 columns. trimAl keeps 148 (135 parsimony-informative).
6. `05_tree_report.py`. Midpoint root, tables and figure.

### Tree

Figure: `analysis/pf28404_family/results/pf28404_tree.png` (PDF beside it). Tree:
`results/pf28404.treefile`.

- The *Coccidioides* copies fall in **four separate groups**. Each group holds one paralog of every
  *Coccidioides* proteome. Each group has its own closest clade of other Onygenales
  (`results/coccidioides_clades.tsv`):

| Group (RS paralog) | Clade tips | UFBoot | Closest non-*Coccidioides* tips |
|---|---|---|---|
| A, PRA3 (CIMG_02492) | 12 | 100 | *Chrysosporium queenslandicum*, *Byssoonygena ceratinophila* |
| B (CIMG_07303) | 13 | 68 | *Emydomyces*, 2 *Chrysosporium*, *Byssoonygena* |
| C (CIMG_05560) | 12 | 79 | *Emydomyces*, *Chrysosporium keratinophilum*, *Aphanoascus* |
| D (CIMG_07843) | 11 | 99 | 2 *Amauroascus* |

- The closest clade of each group holds other Onygenales genera (*Chrysosporium*, *Byssoonygena*,
  *Emydomyces*, *Aphanoascus*, *Amauroascus*). So the split between the four groups is older than
  the genus *Coccidioides*. The four paralogs are not recent duplicates.
- Onygenales tips form 8 maximal Onygenales-only clades (five large ones: 58, 38, 28, 20 and
  19 tips). Ten groups of non-Onygenales tips lie between them. The family has several paralog
  lineages that go back beyond Onygenales. The midpoint root is arbitrary, and the outgroup sample
  does not give a root.
- *Uncinocarpus reesii*, the closest relative of *Coccidioides* in this set, has two copies. By
  patristic distance they are nearest to RS paralogs C (0.56) and A (0.36). Its other two groups
  have no copy in this proteome.
- The three dimorphic-pathogen genera *Histoplasma*, *Blastomyces* and *Paracoccidioides* have 0
  or 1 copy per proteome, on long branches. Nearest RS paralog: D for *Histoplasma*,
  *Blastomyces* and *Emmonsia*; B for *Paracoccidioides*. Distances are 1.4 to 2.0 substitutions
  per site. These assignments are weak (`results/nearest_anchor.tsv`).
- *T. benhamiae* CBS 112371 has two PF28404 proteins: ARB_05178 (F3F37487_006642, 210 aa) and
  F3F37487_005367 (322 aa). *Trichophyton* and *Arthroderma* have 1 to 3 copies per proteome, close
  to the 4 in *Coccidioides*.

## Structure: D4ALI1 and PRA3

Scripts: `analysis/class2b_pra3_fulllength/` on branch `pra3-fulllength` (PR #42). Foldseek 9-427df8a,
databases: pdb100 (250101) and AlphaFold DB Swiss-Prot (v6).

| Query | Confident core (pLDDT >= 70) | Best Swiss-Prot AFDB hit |
|---|---|---|
| PRA3 full length (E9CRM7) | 67 aa, 11 Cys | D4ALI1: qTM 0.74 to 0.82, LDDT 0.74 to 0.76, E 3e-4 |
| D4ALI1 (ARB_05178) | 70 aa | itself only (qTM 1.0). Next: P28797 qTM 0.56 (TM-align), P28799-3 0.48 |

- PRA3 matches ARB_05178, the founder of its own Pfam family. This agrees with the sequence result.
  It does not assign a fold.
- Neither core matches pdb100 above query TM 0.39. All LDDT values are 0.36 or lower. The D4ALI1
  core has no TM-align hit in pdb100.
- Both cores give weak hits to progranulin entries (human P28799, guinea pig P28797) with
  LDDT 0.48 to 0.53 and E 0.3 to 5. These are below the LDDT 0.7 level of the genuine relationships
  in the earlier runs. Progranulin contains Cys-rich granulin domains. Cys-rich small domains often match each other in
  structure search without a common origin. This is not evidence of homology.
- In the AlphaFold model of E9CRM7, all 12 Cys form six SG-SG pairs at 1.99 to 2.04 A. This is a
  property of the model. It shows that a full set of six disulfides fits the model. It is not
  experimental evidence.

## Caveats

- The tree is a protein tree of a short, trimmed alignment (148 columns). Support for groups B and
  C is moderate (68 and 79). The deep relationships between the four groups are not resolved.
- Orthology is not inferred. "Group" means a clade of the tree.
- The outgroup is a sample of orders. The non-Onygenales copy numbers are estimates.
- Copy numbers come from one search with the Pfam GA threshold. A divergent copy below the
  threshold would be missed. A hit in an unannotated region or a fragment would be missed too.
- Gene models of the proteomes were not checked. Fragmented models can add or remove a copy.
- Pangenome strains of *Coccidioides* were not searched. Copy-number variation within the
  genus is not covered.
- No function is inferred for any of these proteins. The family is "uncharacterised" in Pfam.
