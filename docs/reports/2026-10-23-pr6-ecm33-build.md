## 1. What was run

Job 30285974 took the 481 conservative phmmer members of Q5AGC4 (UniRef50 2025_03, E <= 1e-5, fungal, with a fine clade; 19 clades, 11 with at least 5 members and a buildable training set). Job array 30287890 (code `ba15e0c8`, freeze sha256 `a7553076...2ce915`, `--array=0-22%4`, partition `batch`, 8 cores, 16 GB, 12 h per task) built one Ecm33 HMM per held-out clade and independence mode, plus one `all` model: 23 tasks, all COMPLETED exit 0 (56 min to 3 h 04 min), no FATAL in any log. Independence: `cluster30` is the headline (no training member in the same 30 percent identity / 50 percent coverage MMseqs2 cluster as a held-out member); `none` (only the held-out members removed) is a descriptive second column. The report ran locally from the copied-back tables. Every number below was recomputed from the committed TSVs (`hits_*`, `heldout_ids.txt`, `cutoff.json`) and agrees with `report/recall_by_unit.tsv`.

Comparators: Pfam GA (PF12454, `--cut_ga`), relaxed Pfam (PF12454 with a frozen lower cutoff, 15.0 in every unit and mode), and the iteration comparator (jackhmmer -N 3, incE 1e-5, seeded by the training alignment). u is the number of held-out members missed by all three comparators; a unit is testable only if u >= 3 (`min_u`), and then the custom model must recover ceil(u/2) of u.

## 3. What the numbers say

**(a) The custom model hits every held-out member.** Custom hits equal the held-out count in all 22 unit/mode rows (469 of 469 members in the 11 units, counted once per mode; 106 + 55 + 8 + 37 + 38 + 33 + 12 + 5 + 107 + 57 + 11 = 469). The lowest held-out score in any row is 86.9 (Eurotiomycetes, cluster30), against cutoffs of 31.1 to 45.9 (cluster30) and 19.8 to 39.8 (none). Extra calls are 4 or 5 in every row (cluster30: 4 to 5; none: 4 to 5), at most 5 against the limit 16.2455, so all 22 rows are within the limit (`within_limit` yes). Hard-negative call rates are 0.0000 in all 198 group rows, against the frozen 0.05: no group is over the rate.

**(b) Pfam is weaker.** Pfam GA (PF12454) hits 0 of 8 (Glomeromycota), 0 of 37, 0 of 33, 0 of 5, 0 of 107 (Saccharomycotina), 1 of 12, 1 of 11, 6 of 38, 6 of 57, 12 of 55 and 24 of 106 (Dothideomycetes). Relaxed Pfam finds a few more: 0 of 8, 0 of 33, 2 of 37, 3 of 5, 3 of 107, 3 of 12, 3 of 11, 10 of 38, 10 of 57, 27 of 55 and 31 of 106. The custom model beats both Pfam comparators in every unit.

**(c) The iteration comparator also hits every member, so no unit is testable.** The jackhmmer comparator seeded by the training alignment hits every held-out member in every row (iteration hits equal held-out n in all 22 rows), so u = 0 in every unit and both modes. By the freeze's rule (the custom model must recover ceil(u/2) of u, testable only if u >= 3) no unit is testable: 0 of 11 units, in the headline cluster30 mode and in the `none` mode. This is the same outcome as the hydrophobin run (u was 0). The experiment shows that the custom model works and beats Pfam. It does not show that it beats a cheap iterated profile: the freeze's own criterion for worth shipping is neither met nor refuted.

**(d) The independence rule did not matter here.** cluster30 removed 29 to 160 training neighbours per unit (136 for Dothideomycetes, leaving a training set of 239 of 375; 131 for Sordariomycetes, 29 for Glomeromycota). The recall did not change (106 of 106 in both modes) and u is 0 in both. Cutoffs differ between modes (for example Dothideomycetes 32.9 against 19.8), but every held-out member stays far above them.

**(e) Circularity.** The held-out members are the phmmer hits of Q5AGC4 (E <= 1e-5) in the same UniRef50 search that supplied the training members. So 100 percent recall of held-out members says the family is coherent and that a profile transfers across clades. It does not say that the model finds Ecm33 beyond what a similarity search from Q5AGC4 already finds. The same holds for the iteration comparator, which is seeded by the same alignment. Units are clades, not independent clusters.

**(f) The two true Ecm33s among the shared negatives.** P38248 (S. cerevisiae Ecm33) and Q4WNS8 (A. fumigatus ecm33) are left out of the hard-negative rate by the freeze (`negatives_ignored`) and have their own lines (`ignored_negatives_line.tsv`, 11 units x 2 modes each). The custom model hits both in all 22 rows: P38248 scores 314.9 to 407.3 (cluster30) and 337.3 to 397.6 (none) against cutoffs of 31.1 to 45.9 and 19.8 to 39.8 (at least about 7.8 times the cutoff); Q4WNS8 scores 389.1 to 454.2 and 419.2 to 454.5 (at least 9.0 times the cutoff). Relaxed Pfam (cutoff 15.0) finds Q4WNS8 (score 69.0) and has no score for P38248 (it is not a hit of the relaxed table). In the first row of the headline mode (Dothideomycetes): P38248 368.4, Q4WNS8 393.7, cutoff 32.9. Every cell is `descriptive`.

## 4. Caveats

- The members are homologs by search, not curated Ecm33; Kiss ecm33 (Q5AGC4) is row_verified.
- Clade units are related members, not independent clusters; the small units (Pezizomycetes 5, Glomeromycota 8) give little.
- A cutoff set without positive scores (`positives_used: false`) is a rule of the freeze; the margin between held-out scores and cutoffs is large (lowest 86.9 against at most 45.9), so the cutoff choice is not what limits the result.
- Extra calls are counted against the labelled set (phmmer of Q5AGC4 on each proteome, E 1e-5). Four extra calls fall in A. fumigatus in every row; the fifth, present in 6 of the 22 rows, is in C. immitis.
