### 2b. *C. immitis* spherule table (PR #56)

Report: `docs/reports/2026-10-03-cocci-spherule-surface-table.md`. Table:
`analysis/cocci_spherule/spherule_surface_table.tsv.gz` (9,757 genes, 33 columns). Shortlist:
`shortlist_spherule_up.tsv` (564 genes).

- **Gene version.** The existing RS1 kallisto counts use FungiDB-46 `CIMG_` IDs. That annotation is identical
  to NCBI RefSeq GCF_000149335.2 (9,905 genes, all 10,058 transcripts the same). RS2 (`CIMG2_`) and the
  pangenome RS proteome (`C3CED3A_`) are different annotations. No re-run of kallisto was needed.
- DESeq2 on the RS1 counts: 564 genes up at 48 h spherule (log2FC at least 2, padj below 0.05), 36 at
  log2FC at least 6. Only 2 replicates per condition. SOWgp (CIMG_04613) is on top (+9.09). PRA3, PRA2 and
  Ag2/PRA are down.
- SignalP 6 (GPU job) calls 458 of 9,757 genes (4.7%). Only 23 of the 564 spherule-up genes (4.1%) have a
  signal peptide. No enrichment.
- 41 of the 564 are Cys-rich (at least 3.9% Cys, the genome 95th percentile), against about 28 expected at
  5%. Not tested statistically.

### 2c. Follow-up (PR #57)

Report: `docs/reports/2026-10-03-cocci-spherule-followup.md`. 45 follow-up genes: 26 specific extreme genes
without a signal peptide (set A) and 25 Cys-rich spherule-up genes without one (set B), 6 in both.

- **The word "specific" in the table is easy to misread.** It comes from the antigen ranking's flag. That
  flag means "no hit at 30% identity and 50% coverage in one proteome per genus of *Histoplasma*,
  *Blastomyces*, *Paracoccidioides*, *A. fumigatus*, and human". It does not mean "no homolog". I re-tested
  the 45 genes with diamond: 44 of 45 meet the flag's own definition. CIMG_04662 (a P-type ATPase) passes
  because its best hits are 26% to 27% identical. The first report (PR #56) still uses "specific" without
  this definition. Renaming the column is open.
- phmmer (E at most 1e-5, 87 proteomes): 19 of 45 genes have no hit outside *Coccidioides*. Only 3 of them
  also have a second-annotation match and prevalence above 1%: CIMG_13082, CIMG_13230, CIMG_13657. Single
  sequence queries: short proteins have little power.
- Gene models are weak for most of the 45: 5 have a close match in the pangenome RS annotation, 29 none.
  21 are absent from the Fungi5k RS annotation. 13 are in at most 1% of 488 proteomes.
- Phobius calls 42 signal peptides among the 564 spherule-up genes, SignalP 23. 19 are Phobius-only.
  No CFEM domain (PF05730) in any of the 564.
- Cys-rich, no outside hit, Phobius signal peptide (candidates to look at first): CIMG_12808, CIMG_03569,
  CIMG_13082.

## 7. How to work here (additions)

- **Push over HTTPS with the `gh` token.** SSH to GitHub is denied in this shell (no agent). Use:
  `git -c credential.helper= -c "credential.helper=!gh auth git-credential" push https://github.com/stajichlab/adhesionPred.git <branch>:<branch>`.
- **The checkout may be shared.** On 2026-10-03 another session checked out `main` and merged a PR while I
  worked, and my commit landed on `main`. Run `git branch --show-current` before every commit. I moved the
  commit and reset local `main` to the remote. Nothing was pushed to `main`.
- **`gh pr create` can hang** if the command line contains an unclosed here-document. Write the body to a
  file and use `--body-file ... < /dev/null`.
- **GPU queues.** `exfab` and `short_gpu` estimates were unreliable. A job on `short_gpu` was estimated at
  02:40 the next day, then ran within minutes. SignalP 6 (GPU) plus PredGPI on 9,910 proteins took 2 min
  54 s. Do not wait on the estimate.
- **Bash tool:** a foreground `sleep` is blocked. Use `run_in_background` with an `until` loop on `squeue`.
- **Pre-commit** (`ruff`, `ruff-format`) may reformat and fail a commit once. Stage and commit again. It
  also lints curator helper scripts, so those need clean code.
- **Quality checks that worked in the pilots:** hash the candidate list before reading any model score;
  have a different model review every row; compute the counts from the files, not from the curator's text.
  The reviewers' counts differed from the curators' in several places.
- Repo code needs `/usr/bin/python3.12` for Mycelium scripts. SLURM scripts use `$SCRATCH`. The step 1
  feature job is reusable on any protein FASTA: put it at `<workdir>/phaseb/unique_sequences.fasta.gz`
  (`analysis/cocci_spherule/00_prepare_refseq_proteins.py` shows how).
